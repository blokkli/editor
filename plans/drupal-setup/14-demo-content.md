# 14: Demo content

- **Status:** todo
- **Milestone:** M3 Hosted
- **Depends on:** 05, 13

## Goal

The content every sandbox starts with: realistic pages a visitor can relate to
and edit, not a page about blökkli. It replaces chunk 05's placeholder pages.
This chunk comes last (decided 2026-09-26).

## Tasks

- [ ] Decide the topic. Criteria:
  - a genre everyone knows, so obvious edits come to mind
  - content that needs the blocks (tables, charts, galleries, video, a map,
    reused boxes, interlinked pages)
  - plausibly trilingual
  - images that may be redistributed in the repo and the public sandbox
  - no dates that go stale; a fictional organisation
- [ ] Bespoke paragraph types and fields the content needs (e.g. caption and
      copyright on images, quotes, tables).
- [ ] The pages in EN, with DE and FR translations, some deliberately outdated.
- [ ] Images and other media, with their licences recorded.
- [ ] Suggested tasks for the sandbox's welcome panel (chunk 09).

## Acceptance criteria

- `ddev bake` builds the template from this content.
- Every page renders in all three languages.

## Notes

- **Candidate (2026-09-26): a fictional museum.**
  - Museums' open-access collections (the Met, Rijksmuseum, Art Institute of

  - Pages: home, an exhibition, visit (prices, hours, map, questions and
    answers), "the museum in numbers" (charts), a journal article.
  - It fits scheduling (the next exhibition, a holiday closure), comments and
