### Improvements

#### The AI assistant sees your selection from the very first message

When you select a block and immediately ask the AI assistant something like
"Convert this into an accordion", it now knows right away what you mean.
Previously it often had to ask back or look up the selection in a separate step.

#### The AI assistant handles nested blocks correctly

When the AI assistant creates a structure made of several blocks, it now
reliably understands which blocks sit inside which. Previously it sometimes
misread its own additions and partially deleted what it had just created.

#### Other improvements

- The list of changes that the AI assistant proposes for confirmation can now be
  navigated entirely with the keyboard.
